# Asset provenance

All runtime assets are bundled locally. The application makes no runtime CDN or
third-party asset requests.

## Project-generated assets

The seven terrestrial exterior vistas and six decorative-art atlases in
`art-source/generated` were generated for this project. Their prompts and
intended uses are recorded in `art-source/generated/prompts.json`. World
geometry, LODs, collision metadata, book atlases, and runtime material
derivatives are generated by project scripts. World Atlas previews in
`art-source/previews` are direct captures of the bundled WebGL scenes and are
processed into compact runtime WebP files by `pnpm assets:previews`. Matching
mobile scene baselines are stored under `art-source/baselines/mobile`.

The Alexandria harbor vista and astronomical fresco were generated locally
for the Alexandrian Mouseion world. They are bundled as project assets and do
not make a runtime request.

## NASA reference imagery

The Lunar South Pole Archive derives its local lunar color and displacement
maps from NASA Scientific Visualization Studio's LRO WAC color mosaic and
LOLA/Kaguya terrain products. Its Earth texture derives from NASA's Blue
Marble dataset. Source pages, direct asset URLs, credits, hashes, and processed
derivatives are locked in `public/worlds/assets/exteriors/space-assets.json`.
NASA states that its content generally is not subject to copyright in the
United States, while its identifiers and imagery remain subject to the
[NASA Images and Media Usage Guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/).
These reference textures are used with the source credits recorded in the
manifest, without NASA logos or an implication of endorsement, and are bundled
locally; no NASA request is made at runtime.

Arkship nebula and planet maps are deterministic project-generated textures
created by `scripts/process-space-assets.mjs`.

Exact source URLs, authors, source hashes, processed derivatives, and file
sizes are recorded in `art-source/asset-lock.json`. Poly Haven and ambientCG
both publish their listed assets under CC0 1.0.
